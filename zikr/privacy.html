Zikr: privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Zikr is a remembrance (dhikr) counter with Hisn al-Muslim, the Quran, prayer times and the adhan. It is built to keep what you do on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your zikr list, counts, reflections, Quran reading position and bookmarks, and settings are saved only on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used only to work out prayer times and to set the adhan for where you are. It is used on the phone and is not sent anywhere. You can choose a city by hand instead.

Notifications

The adhan notifications are scheduled on your phone for your prayer times. Nothing is sent to a server.

Network

The Quran, Hisn al-Muslim, hadith, tafsir and the adhan recording are built into the app. The app may load fonts from Google Fonts; that request does not include anything about you.
